Privacy at Community Bridge

Free four-language community-resource finder for Indian River County, FL — English, Spanish, Haitian Creole and Portuguese.

Find community resources without an account or payment.

Typed searches are processed in your browser and are not sent to Community Bridge. If you use voice search, your browser's speech-recognition provider may process your audio. If you enable location access, your browser or device location service may process your location. Community Bridge does not store these inputs or transmit them to its own servers.

Hosting note: this site is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may keep standard security logs of visits, such as IP addresses. Those logs belong to GitHub — Community Bridge does not receive or use them.

Analytics note: this app does not collect or report visitor counts, search counts, or referral counts. There is no in-app analytics, and no usage numbers are promised to anyone.

Community Bridge is a free community directory — no account, no cost. For local help anywhere, call 211.
